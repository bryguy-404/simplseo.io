01 · educational

Helpful even before Google is connected

Isolated example
Example Roofing · questionI haven’t connected Google yet. What makes a good page title?
SEO Mentor · example answer

Make the title clearly describe what someone will find on that page.

Use a short, specific description of the service. Include your location if it is relevant and accurate. Avoid repeating keywords or using the same generic title everywhere.

Google recommends descriptive, concise titles and may choose different wording for the title shown in search. Google’s title guidance

Useful next stepShare the page’s current title and the service it describes. We can check whether the wording is accurate and easy to understand.
Sources and evidence (1)
  • Google Search Central: Influencing your title links

Official guidance reviewed for this static preview on October 11, 2026. The suggested next step is SimplSEO’s interpretation; no account metrics were needed.
