Your page’s progress at a glance

Isolated examples — no live account data. These are labeled historical examples, not results for Bryan Develops. Website editing stays manual.

1. Collecting data2. More search activity3. Numbers moved down4. More evidence needed

1. Collecting data

An elapsed-time bar shows how long the page has been tracked and when the first comparison is expected. Reaching that date does not invent a result.

2. More search activity

Clicks, appearances and average position are visible together before a review. A lower average-position number is better.

3. Numbers moved down

Words and arrows describe a decline as well as color. A review helps choose the next step; these numbers alone do not prove why it happened.

4. More evidence needed

Small counts remain visible, with a clear caution and the saved next step. Missing observations are shown as unavailable, never as zero traffic.

Earlier changes and dated results stay saved. Checking progress, reviewing results, or accepting tasks does not restart tracking.
