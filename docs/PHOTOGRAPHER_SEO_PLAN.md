## Audience and objective

Start with one photography page covering wedding and portrait photographers. Prioritize owners who maintain their own websites, want to understand SEO, and will implement recommendations. An existing website with some Google Search Console history is a useful qualification; the owner's age is not the targeting criterion.

The page should turn relevant visitors into **qualified requests for free early access**. A useful early user connects their website, understands a recommendation, and makes a real improvement. Requests alone do not establish product fit or willingness to pay.

The software remains useful to other businesses. Photography is the initial marketing focus Bryan selected. This document records the research, accepted direction, and subsequent implementation. No outreach has been performed.

### Page sections

1. **Hero and product explanation.** Identify the audience, explain the owner-led workflow, and offer the invitation CTA. Match the current SimplSEO visual style and keep the copy readable on mobile.
2. **See one page improvement from start to finish.** Show a real, permitted walkthrough: the page being reviewed, available evidence, a recommendation, where the owner makes the edit, and how later observations are displayed. If a demonstration uses sample data, label it as an illustration. Show an honest waiting or low-data state when there is no measured result.
3. **SEO for wedding photographers.** Use a wedding service page as the example. Illustrate reviewing whether the page clearly describes the actual service and area served when the evidence supports that recommendation. Do not promise a particular keyword, ranking, or booking increase.
4. **SEO for portrait photographers.** Use a family portrait service page as the example. Demonstrate making an existing service page clearer to prospective clients. Family portraits illustrate the portrait specialty; they do not require another landing page at launch.
5. **How it works.** Connect your website and Search Console, review a page, choose a useful change, publish it in your own editor, and follow the available observations. Make clear that the owner does the implementation and that a recommendation can be to leave a page unchanged.
6. **Questions before joining.** Answer the practical questions below in concise visible text.
7. **Closing invitation.** Repeat the primary CTA and early-access terms. Invite feedback from photographers managing their own sites.

Use a product screenshot or walkthrough as the main evidence. A decorative photography image should not replace the product explanation. Do not manufacture customer quotes, photographer adoption counts, before-and-after traffic charts, or time-saving claims.

## Technical launch plan

Read-only checks on November 25 found that `/` resolves to `/home`, which returns 200 and declares `https://simpl-seo.com/home` as its canonical URL. The standard `/robots.txt` and `/sitemap.xml` URLs both returned 404; corresponding route files were not found in the repository search.

A missing robots.txt does not itself block Google: Google treats a 404 as no crawl restrictions. A sitemap helps discovery but is not required for every small, well-linked site and does not guarantee indexing. [Google's robots.txt behavior](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/robots-txt/robots-txt-spec), [Google's sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/overview).

The implementation should:

- Add a public `/seo-for-photographers` route with its own title, description, canonical, and social preview. Keep the important text available in the HTML without login.
- Add a clear link from `/home`, and include contextual links from future tutorials. Do not change the homepage canonical as part of this page launch.
- Add a sitemap of intended public canonical pages and a robots.txt that permits discovery of the marketing content and references that sitemap. Keep private application URLs out of the sitemap; review existing indexing controls before changing them.
- Compress and size images appropriately, supply useful alternative text where needed, and keep navigation, FAQ controls, and CTAs accessible by keyboard and on mobile.
- Verify the existing invitation journey and all advertised features with the intended early-access account type before publishing the claims. Changing pilot eligibility would be a separate implementation decision.
- After publication, inspect the URL in Search Console and submit the sitemap in the available webmaster accounts. Record what each tool actually reports; an HTTP 200 is not proof of indexing.
